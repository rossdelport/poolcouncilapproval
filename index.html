Common questions

Do I need council approval for a pool in Victoria?

Yes. In Victoria a building permit is required for any swimming pool or spa that can hold more than 300 mm (30 cm) of water. This includes above-ground pools, spas and many relocatable pools.

How long does pool council approval take?

A standard pool building permit usually takes a few weeks once all documents are ready. Sites with planning overlays, easements or heritage controls can take longer. We give you a timeline with your quote.

Can you help if my pool was built without a permit?

Yes. We arrange retrospective (after the fact) building permits so your pool can be registered and certified as compliant.
