What is a retrospective pool permit?

In Western Australia, a retrospective permit is called a Building Approval Certificate. It is approval for a pool or spa that was built without one. It is common when people buy a home with an older pool, or when a spa was installed without anyone realising a permit was needed.

Why it matters

  • All pools and spas in WA over 30 cm deep must have a compliant barrier, and councils inspect them at least every four years
  • Unapproved pools can cause problems when you sell your home
  • Councils can issue fines and orders for unapproved or non-compliant pools
  • Your home insurance may be affected if something goes wrong

How we fix it

  • Site inspection and review of what was built
  • Plans and engineering certification of the existing pool
  • Certificate of Building Compliance from a private building surveyor
  • Advice on any barrier upgrades needed (and who can do them)
  • Lodging the Building Approval Certificate application with council

FAQs

Common questions

Will I get in trouble for an unapproved pool?

Taking action to get it approved is the best step. We work with you and the building surveyor to bring the pool into line as smoothly as possible.

Can I sell a house with an unapproved pool?

You can, but it often causes delays and price negotiations. Getting the pool approved first usually makes the sale smoother.
