The approval process

How Pool Council Approval Works

Here is exactly what happens, step by step, when you get your pool or spa approved with us.

Get a free quote Call 0400 369 865

Pools & spas onlyFixed, upfront pricingCertified applicationsPerth & regional WA

  1. Free quote

    Send us your address and a few details about the pool or spa. We check your council, zoning, R-Codes and any planning controls and give you a fixed price.

  2. Plans and documents

    We prepare the site plan, engineering, barrier details and the Certificate of Design Compliance your council needs.

  3. Permits lodged

    We lodge your building permit (and development approval if needed) with your local council and deal with them for you.

  4. Inspections

    We help you get the safety barrier right so it passes the council pool inspection first time.

  5. Approved and compliant

    Your pool passes its barrier inspection and you have all the paperwork to prove it is approved. Perfect for peace of mind and when you sell.
