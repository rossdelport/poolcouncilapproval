How Pool Council Approval Works

Here is exactly what happens, step by step, when you get your pool or spa approved with us.

Get a free quote Call 0400 000 000

  1. Free quote

    Send us your address and a few details about the pool or spa. We check your council, zoning and overlays and give you a fixed price.

  2. Plans and documents

    We prepare or review the site plan, engineering, barrier details and everything the building surveyor needs.

  3. Permits lodged

    We lodge your building permit (and planning permit if needed) and deal with the surveyor and council for you.

  4. Inspections

    We book the required inspections during and after construction, including the safety barrier inspection.

  5. Registered and compliant

    You get your certificate of final inspection, your pool is registered with council and your barrier certificate is lodged.
