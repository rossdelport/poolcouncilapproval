Common questions

Do I need a permit for a pool in Victoria?

Yes. Any pool or spa that can hold more than 300 mm (30 cm) of water needs a building permit, a compliant safety barrier and council registration.

Do above-ground pools need a permit?

Yes, if they can hold more than 30 cm of water. Above-ground pools must also have a compliant barrier. The pool walls can sometimes form part of the barrier if they are high enough and not climbable.

Do inflatable pools need a permit?

Small inflatable pools that hold less than 30 cm of water do not. Larger inflatable or relocatable pools that hold more than 30 cm, and are set up for three days or more, must be registered and fenced.

How long does it take to get a pool permit?

Usually a few weeks once all documents are ready. Planning overlays, easements or heritage controls can add time.

What is a certificate of final inspection?

It is the document the building surveyor issues when the pool and barrier have passed the final inspection. You need it to register the pool with council.

Do I need a planning permit for a pool?

Most pools do not, but you may need one if your land has a heritage, vegetation, flood or bushfire overlay, or other planning controls. We check this for you.

What is pool registration?

All Victorian pools and spas over 30 cm deep must be registered with the local council. Councils keep a register so they can make sure barriers are inspected regularly.

How often do pool fences need to be inspected?

Every four years. A registered inspector checks the barrier and issues a certificate of barrier compliance, which is lodged with council.

Can a pool be close to the boundary fence?

Often yes, but setbacks, easements and the barrier design all matter. Boundary fences used as part of the pool barrier must meet the pool fencing rules on the pool side.

Do you work with pool builders?

Yes. We handle permits for pool builders across Victoria so they can focus on construction.

What areas do you cover?

All of Victoria, including Melbourne, Geelong, the Mornington Peninsula, Ballarat, Bendigo and regional areas.
