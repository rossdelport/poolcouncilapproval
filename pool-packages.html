Questions.

Who supplies the pool?

Your pool is a genuine Tarter USA galvanised stock tank from Terra Tanks. We buy it for you at Terra Tanks' price, and it's included in your package.

Do I need council approval for a stock tank pool?

Yes. In WA a pool that can hold more than 300 mm of water needs a building permit and a compliant pool barrier. Approval is included in every package, and it's our specialty.

What if my yard needs more than 10 metres of fence?

Many yards can use existing boundary fences as part of the pool barrier. If you need more, we'll price the extra metres clearly at your site visit, before anything is locked in.

Do I pay anything online?

No. Designing your pool and getting a quote is free. We confirm everything at a free site visit, then send a written agreement and invoice.

Which areas do you cover?

Perth metro for now. Call 0400 369 865 if you're just outside it.

Can I see it in person?

Yes. We can bring the designer on an iPad and walk you through every option in your own backyard.
